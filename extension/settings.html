ChronoTrack Privacy Information

• Website visit records are stored in your configured MongoDB backend.
• Disabling Master Tracking prevents new visits from being submitted.
• Disabling Incognito Tracking prevents new Incognito visits from being recorded.
• Settings are stored locally using Chrome Storage.

Note: Existing history records are not automatically deleted when tracking is disabled.
